Counts the Reels and Shorts you watch — with Hoot by your side.

## About

ScrollScope helps you stay aware of how much time you spend watching short-form video without needing a login, cloud account, or complicated setup. It is built to keep things simple, personal, and private.

Hoot the owl adds a friendly touch to the experience while helping you keep track of your habits in a calm, mindful way.
